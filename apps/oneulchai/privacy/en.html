Privacy Policy

Your records stay close,
our practices stay clear

DayDelta stores and analyzes your daily records primarily on your device. Where external services such as ads are needed, we explain what they handle.

Effective date · To be finalized at launch

01

Information DayDelta handles

We handle information you enter or that the app creates to support your daily records.

  • Mood, energy, and stress scores from predictions and reflections
  • Selected areas and items, including your custom areas and items
  • Daily notes and analysis results created on your device
  • Daily cards, card decks, upright/reversed positions, and card encounters
  • App settings such as theme, reminders and times, and onboarding completion
02

Your daily records are processed on your device

DayDelta does not require an account or use its own account server, and Android automatic cloud backup is disabled.

  • Daily records are normally stored on your device.
  • Korean-language analysis of daily notes and pattern calculations also take place on your device.
  • The developer does not transmit or sell your original daily record text to its own servers.
03

How Google Mobile Ads handles information

On screens showing ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process certain device and usage information to serve ads and support quality and security.

  • IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location)
  • Interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad views
  • Performance and diagnostic information from the app and ad SDK
  • Device/account identifiers such as Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Information sent to Google is processed under its policies and settings and encrypted in transit.
04

Backup and restore

Backups are created only when you request them.

  • Backups can include daily records, daily cards, custom areas and items, decorations, sticker inventory, star fragments, and workshop activity.
  • App settings including theme, reminders, selected deck, onboarding completion, and pattern-ad viewing history are backed up. Built-in master data and Google Play purchase entitlements are not.
  • A DayDelta backup file is made only when you explicitly choose Back up.
  • Files are saved to the location you select in the Android file picker.
  • If you choose cloud storage such as Google Drive, the provider may transfer and retain the file under its policies.
  • Restoring returns records, decorations, star fragments, workshop history, and app settings to the backup state. Google Play entitlements are verified separately.
05

Third parties and external services

The developer does not provide your daily records to advertisers.

  • Google Mobile Ads may send the device and usage information described above to Google to serve ads.
  • If you select an external cloud provider for backup, that provider may process the backup file.
06

Retention and deletion

Daily records stay on your device until you delete them.

  • Delete all records removes predictions, reflections, daily cards, analysis results, record/report/card-history data, and pattern decorations. Star fragments, owned items, and app settings remain.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its Android app data deletes the data stored in the app on your device.
  • Android automatic cloud backup is disabled. Device-to-device transfer provided by some operating systems or manufacturers may still behave according to their policies.
  • Backup files you created separately are not erased when app data is deleted. Delete them from their saved location yourself.
  • Retention and deletion of information processed by Google Mobile Ads follow the relevant Google policies.
07

Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, delete, and back up records in DayDelta.

  • You can view and edit prediction and reflection records directly in the app.
  • You can delete all records or run backup and restore yourself.
  • Reminders can be turned off in app settings; permissions can be changed in Android settings.
  • Advertising identifiers and related choices are managed in Android and Google ad settings.
08

Steps to keep your data safe

We use only necessary information and process daily records on your device whenever possible.

  • DayDelta does not operate a remote server for storing your original daily records.
  • Backups and restores run only when you explicitly request them.
  • Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit as described by Google.
